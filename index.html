Subway Sandwiches Calorie Count Canada 2026

Here's something most people get wrong: they pick their protein first and assume that decides the calorie count. It doesn't. Your total sandwich calories equal bread plus protein plus cheese plus veggies plus sauce. Each of these layers has its own nutritional profile. Veggies are essentially free — most add 0 to 5 calories per topping. Bread and protein form the base. Cheese adds about 40 to 60 calories.

And sauces are the wild card, ranging from 0 calories for vinegar to 110 calories for mayo per serving. A smart habit used by calorie-conscious eaters is to build your order starting with protein, then choose your bread, and select your sauce last — not the other way around. This small shift in thinking can realistically save you 200 to 300 calories on a single order.

Combos are convenient, but they're not always the best value at Subway Canada. Subway combo meals include a sandwich, a side like chips or a cookie, and a drink, and range from about CAD $12 up to CAD $18 depending on the sub and extras.

Subway Bread Options and Calorie Count Canada 2026

Your bread choice quietly does a lot of work on your calorie total. Most people grab Italian white out of habit without realizing it's not the lowest option available. Flatbread at 190 calories per 6-inch is the lowest-calorie option and works well with lean proteins and lots of vegetables.

9-Grain Wheat at 210 calories is a better fiber choice than white bread and is ideal for a balanced everyday sandwich. Italian Herbs & Cheese at 240 calories is higher in calories but delivers strong taste and a crisp texture when toasted.

How to Check Subway Canada Menu Prices Near You

Prices at Subway can vary between locations, so it's worth checking before you head in. The Subway Canada app often shows slightly different pricing than what you'll see on the in-store menu board — checking it before you order is a small habit that helps you budget accurately and catch current deals.

Here's how to find the most accurate local pricing:

Step 1: Download the Subway Canada app or visit the official Subway Canada website.

Step 2: Enter your postal code or allow location access to find your nearest location.

Step 3: Tap on your local restaurant to view the menu with prices specific to that location.

Step 4: Check the "Deals" or "Offers" tab before placing your order. Promotional pricing and app-exclusive deals are often listed here and are not always advertised in-store.

Step 5: If you're ordering for delivery through Uber Eats or DoorDash, expect a 10 to 25 percent markup on menu prices plus service and delivery fees on top of the standard menu price.

FAQs - Subway Sandwiches Menu
